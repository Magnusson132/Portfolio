Galaxy Strike

A laser-blasting rail shooter through a vast space world, inspired by Nintendo's Star Fox.

Engine
Unity
Team
Solo, spare-time project
Players
One, or two on the same ship
Status
In development

On rails

Steer and shoot as a spaceship flying through a big, open world. The ship flies forward on its own while the player mostly moves it up, down and sideways.

I used Unity's Animator and Timeline to put the ship on that rail, and Timeline again to spawn enemies at set times. The laser is built from particle effects, aimed with either the mouse or a gamepad.

Two pilots, one ship

One feature I'm happy with: two people can play on the same ship. One steers with a keyboard or controller, and the other aims and shoots with a controller or mouse. Perfect for couch co-op.

Terrain tool

This was the game that taught me Unity's Terrain tool for the first time. What an amazing tool, and so much fun to work with.
