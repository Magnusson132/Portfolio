Hollow Maze

A first-person co-op puzzle game for two players.

Engine
Unreal Engine 5
Team
Four developers
My part
UI, narrative and audio

Two players wake up in separate cells and can never reach each other. One of you sees what the other needs, so you solve the puzzles by talking: light, mirrors, buttons, colours, and a final room that one player rotates while the other guides a beam of light.

What I do

I make games through programming and level design, and I write the music for them too.

Coding

I keep systems decoupled and name things clearly, so teammates can work in my scripts without tripping over them. It pays off on solo projects too.

Level design

I usually come up with my own game ideas and block them out with placeholder assets. In a team, I'd rather share ideas and build something bigger together.

Version control

I'm comfortable with Git and the tools around it. In group projects I've spotted early warning signs several times and restored repositories from backups or recovery branches to save the work.

Team collaboration

I'm used to agile teams: daily check-ins, a shared task board, and saying early when something is blocked so it never grows into a bigger problem.

I work in Unity and Unreal Engine 5, write C# and C++, and keep everything in Git.
