A classic in a new dimension

I made a remake of the classic game Frogger. It's built in Unity and plays out in a 3D environment instead of 2D, with the same kind of obstacles and feel so it keeps its core identity.

Cross the road, hop across the logs and lily pads, and beat your best time.

Menus and high scores

The game has a main menu with credits and a high score list, so there's always a reason for one more run.
